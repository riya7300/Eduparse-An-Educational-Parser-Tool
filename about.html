Welcome to Eduparse

Eduparse is a comprehensive educational tool designed to help students and developers understand the inner workings of LR parsing. Whether you're studying for an exam or building a compiler, Eduparse provides visual insights into how different parsing algorithms process context-free grammars.

LR(0) - Left-to-Right, Rightmost Derivation In Reverse (0 Lookahead)

The simplest form of LR parsing. It constructs a state machine based on "items" but doesn't use any lookahead symbols to decide between shifting or reducing. It's often too restrictive for complex languages.

SLR(1) - Simple LR (1 Lookahead)

An improvement over LR(0) that uses the FOLLOW set of a non-terminal to decide when to perform a reduction. This helps resolve many shift-reduce conflicts found in LR(0).

CLR(1) - Canonical LR (1 Lookahead)

The most powerful LR parsing technique. It keeps track of specific lookahead symbols for every item in each state. While highly capable, it often results in very large parsing tables.

LALR(1) - Look-Ahead LR (1 Lookahead)

The industry standard for parser generators (like Yacc and Bison). It provides a balance by merging states in CLR(1) that have the same core productions, significantly reducing the table size while maintaining most of CLR's power.
